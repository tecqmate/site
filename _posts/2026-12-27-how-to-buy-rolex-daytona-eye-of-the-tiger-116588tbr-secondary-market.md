## Aftermarket "Tiger" Dial Swaps and Custom Bezel Modifications
* **What is the problem?** Dishonest grey-market dealers take standard yellow gold Daytona references (like Ref. 116508 or 116518), drill the bezel, install aftermarket diamond baguettes, and drop in third-party tiger-print dials.
* **Why does it happen?** An authentic factory 116588TBR commands a massive premium over standard yellow gold Daytonas. Third-party conversions cost a fraction of the price to build, allowing bad actors to pocket enormous profit margins by selling modified pieces as genuine factory stock.
