## High-Vis Orange Meets Integrated Chronograph Power

The **Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Offshore Selfwinding Chronograph 42mm Reference 26238ST** with **Orange Accents** is one of the most high-impact variations in the modern Offshore collection. Featuring a classic black *Méga Tapisserie* dial elevated by high-visibility orange tachymeter scale markers, orange chronograph sub-dial rings, and matching rubber strap options, this reference combines rugged stainless steel architecture with bold motorsport-inspired styling.


Because this specific color combination commands high demand among AP collectors bypassing boutique waitlists, the secondary market sees significant activity. ## Dial Modification and Replica Movement Calibers
* **What is the problem?** Fraudulent secondary sellers often take baseline black dial 26238ST models, remove the dial, and apply aftermarket orange paint printing to simulate rare factory configurations.
* **Why does it happen?** The price delta between a standard secondary Offshore and a highly sought-after factory colorway can reach tens of thousands of dollars, tempting unscrupulous dealers into third-party alterations.

## A 4-Step Secondary Market Buying Protocol

If you are committing serious capital to acquire an AP Offshore Reference 26238ST with Orange Accents, enforce this strict buyer-protection workflow:

* **Step 1: Conduct Digital NFC Warranty Verification:** Modern Audemars Piguet watches come with an encrypted NFC smart card warranty. Have the seller scan the card via the official AP portal or confirm the serial number directly with an AP boutique to verify factory dial color records.
* **Step 2: Mandate Physical Inspection via Independent Master Watchmaker:** Never rely exclusively on high-resolution seller photos. Use an accredited third-party watchmaker to inspect caseback seals, movement serial numbers, and column-wheel mechanism operation.
* **Step 3: Test the Quick-Release Interchangeable Strap System:** Modern 26238ST models feature AP's proprietary quick-change strap mechanism embedded on the case lugs. Ensure the buttons release cleanly and straps lock firmly into place.
* **Step 4: Verify Full Set Provenance & Box Set:** Ensure the presentation includes the original AP green wooden box, warranty smart card, instruction manual, and matching extra OEM straps.
