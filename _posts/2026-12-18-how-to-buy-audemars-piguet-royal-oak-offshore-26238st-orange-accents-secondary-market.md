## A 4-Step Secondary Market Buying Protocol

If you are committing serious capital to acquire an AP Offshore Reference 26238ST with Orange Accents, enforce this strict buyer-protection workflow:

## Due-Diligence Questions Before You Commit

- **Can the seller show the factory dial record?** Ask for an official warranty confirmation that names this colourway, not just a photograph of a card.
- **Which strap options are included?** Request an itemised list of the rubber and leather straps, clasp, and quick-release components.
- **Has the chronograph been serviced?** Ask who last serviced the column-wheel mechanism and whether any pushers, seals, or hands were replaced.
- **What is the condition of the case?** Ask directly whether the bevels have been refinished and request macro photographs in neutral light.
- **Are all serials consistent?** Ask for photographs of the case serial and warranty record and confirm they agree.

## Common Mistakes and FAQ

**Is a bright orange dial automatically factory original?** No. Ask for documentation and specialist review; aftermarket printing can look convincing in photos.

**Does an NFC scan remove the need for a physical inspection?** No. Treat it as one check among movement, case, and strap verification.

**What if the seller has only one strap?** Ask whether other straps were never supplied or have been omitted; either way, record it in the condition notes.
