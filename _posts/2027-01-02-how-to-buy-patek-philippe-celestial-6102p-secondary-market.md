## The Night Sky of Geneva on Your Wrist

The **Patek Philippe Celestial Reference 6102P-001** in solid platinum is one of the most poetically complex timepieces ever developed by the legendary Geneva manufacture. Featuring a deep blue dial composed of three sapphire crystal discs sliding above one another, the 6102P displays an accurate sky chart of the Northern Hemisphere, tracking the nocturnal sky, the phases and orbit of the moon, and the meridian transit times of Sirius and the moon.

Housed in a substantial 44mm platinum case decorated with hand-engraved Calatrava cross motifs along the case flanks, the 6102P is powered by the ultra-thin automatic Calibre 240 LU CL C featuring an off-center 22k gold micro-rotor. Commanding mid-six-figure to seven-figure valuations on the private secondary market, acquiring a Ref. 6102P requires strict forensic auditing to guard against counterfeit printed dials, damaged sapphire discs, and missing archive documentation.

## Printed Fake Discs, Misaligned Chart Discs, and Missing Provenance
* **What is the problem?** Private secondary-market buyers encounter counterfeit models utilizing static printed plastic dials instead of multi-layered metallized sapphire crystal discs, as well as genuine examples with jammed moon phase gearing or altered case back serial numbers.
* **Why does it happen?** The multi-layered sapphire disc system requires microscopic precision and specialized optical coatings to function. Replica factories substitute printed film or acrylic to simulate the celestial chart at a tiny fraction of the cost.
