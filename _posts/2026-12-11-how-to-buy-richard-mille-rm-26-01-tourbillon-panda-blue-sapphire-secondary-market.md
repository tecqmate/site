## 2. Tinted Resin Replicas and Aftermarket Gem Swaps
* **What is the problem?** Illicit manufacturing operations attempt to mimic blue sapphire crystal cases using tinted acrylic or glass composites, while placing low-grade cast metal panda figures over standard non-tourbillon movements.
* **Why does it happen?** The colossal price tag of a 1-of-1 Richard Mille high-jewelry tourbillon creates massive financial incentive to present custom aftermarket assemblies as factory-original pieces.

## Choose the Right Variant: Blue vs Green Sapphire

The blue and green RM 26-01 Panda piece uniques share the same hand-engraved white gold panda, pave diamonds, black sapphires, and yellow gold bamboo; the real distinction is the **blue sapphire crystal case**, whose hue comes from trace elements, against the green case of its sibling. That colour difference should be reflected in the warranty documentation and factory archive record for the specific movement serial. Both are 1-of-1 assets, so liquidity is often highly personal and slow rather than comparable to a series-produced reference. For the blue variant, pay particular attention to Mohs hardness testing, internal gas bubbles under UV, and whether the leather-bound certificate cites the blue case alongside the matching serial.

## Due-Diligence Questions for the Blue Piece Unique

- **Which case specification is named on the certificate?** Ask the seller to show the exact wording for the blue sapphire case and the matching movement serial.
- **Can the factory record be checked independently?** Request confirmation through an authorised boutique or archive channel before funds are committed.
- **What is known about the panda sculpture?** Ask who executed the setting and whether any stones have been replaced or repaired.
- **Has the tourbillon been serviced?** Request workshop invoices and confirm that the cage is functional rather than decorative.
- **Is the full leather box and document set present?** Ask for a photographed inventory of certificate, manual, straps, and fittings.

## Common Mistakes and FAQ

**Does blue tint prove sapphire?** No. Tinted glass and resin can appear similar; ask for non-destructive testing by a qualified specialist.

**Can a certificate alone prove one-of-one status?** It should be cross-checked against factory records and the physical serials.

**Why is liquidity a concern?** Single-piece assets often have a thin buyer pool, so compare them as individual works rather than interchangeable holdings.
