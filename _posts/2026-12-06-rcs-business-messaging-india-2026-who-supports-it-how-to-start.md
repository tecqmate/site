For years, RCS (Rich Communication Services) in India was an Android story. In September 2026 that changed: Jio began offering commercial RCS business messaging to iPhone users on its network, with a new pricing structure for businesses.

This guide explains what RCS business messaging is, who can receive it, how it compares to SMS and WhatsApp, and how to get started without wasting money.

## Quick answer

RCS business messaging lets a verified brand send rich, interactive messages (images, cards, buttons) to a customer's **default messaging app**, with no separate app to install. It is worth testing if many of your customers use Android or Jio iPhones and you want richer messages than SMS. Support, pricing and rules differ by operator and are still changing, so **start with a small pilot and keep SMS as a fallback**.

## What RCS business messaging is

RCS is the upgrade to SMS. Instead of plain text, a business can send:

- Branded messages with a verified sender name and logo
- Images, short videos and carousels of cards
- Buttons for quick replies, links, calls, maps or calendar events
- Order, booking and support conversations inside the messaging app

Customers do not install anything, because RCS lives in the default messaging app on supported phones. For a general comparison with other channels, see our guide on [SMS vs WhatsApp vs RCS]({{ '/2026/11/16/sms-vs-whatsapp-vs-rcs-business-messaging-india/' | relative_url }}).

## What changed in 2026

According to industry reporting from September 2026:

- **Jio began commercial RCS business messaging support for iPhone users** on its network. Prices for businesses were introduced from 1 September 2026.
- **Pricing moved to a use-case model**, with separate charges for marketing, transactional and OTP messages, similar in spirit to how WhatsApp prices by category.
- One industry analysis said Jio's pricing was substantially higher than its earlier RCS pricing. Check current rates with your provider rather than relying on reports.
- Apple already supports business RCS in several other countries, and India is now added through Jio.
- Jio has described itself as the exclusive RCS provider for iOS in India. Other operators were reported as likely to follow, but their status may change.

Reports on Airtel and Vodafone Idea differ in detail and date, so confirm each operator's current status with your messaging provider.

## Who can receive your RCS messages

| Audience | Situation |
| --- | --- |
| Android users | RCS is supported on most modern Android phones through the default messaging app, if RCS chat features are turned on |
| iPhone users on Jio | Business RCS is now available, on supported iOS versions with RCS turned on |
| iPhone users on other networks | Status depends on the operator, so check before you plan |

On iPhone, users can switch RCS business messages on or off in their messaging settings, so not every eligible customer will receive them. Expect your reach to be lower than the number of phones in your list.

## Rules and requirements

Rules for RCS are set by operators and the platforms that connect them, and they are still developing. In general, expect:

- **Verified sender approval.** You register a brand or agent and prove you are who you say you are.
- **Registered use case.** Messages must match the purpose you registered, such as transactional, OTP or promotional. Do not stray from it.
- **Consent.** Obtain and record consent before sending promotional messages. See our guide on [consent for SMS and WhatsApp]({{ '/2026/11/30/customer-consent-sms-whatsapp-india-trai-dlt-dpdp/' | relative_url }}).
- **Regulatory developments.** TRAI amended its commercial messaging rules in 2026. Read our summary of the [TCCCPR Third Amendment]({{ '/2026/12/03/trai-tcccpr-third-amendment-2026-what-changed-business-checklist/' | relative_url }}) and check how they apply to your channels.
- **No deceptive design.** Messages should be clear about who is sending and why.

## What it costs

Pricing varies by operator and use case, and changes often. Typical questions to ask your provider:

- What is the price per message for marketing, transactional and OTP use cases?
- Are failed or undelivered messages charged?
- Is there a fee for agent setup, verification or monthly platform use?
- What is charged for fallback SMS?
- How will price changes be communicated?

Compare total cost per **successful, useful message**, not just the headline rate. Our [checklist for choosing a CPaaS or SMS provider]({{ '/2026/11/24/how-to-choose-cpaas-sms-provider-india-checklist/' | relative_url }}) explains how to compare providers properly.

## How to get started

1. **Decide the use case.** Order updates, bookings, offers or support are good starting points. Pick one.
2. **Check your audience.** Look at the phones and operators your customers use, to estimate how many can receive RCS.
3. **Choose a provider that supports RCS** and ask which operators and iOS support they offer today.
4. **Register your brand or agent.** Prepare your business details, logo, brand colour, description and use case.
5. **Design a simple message flow.** One clear message, one or two buttons, and a plain fallback text.
6. **Set up SMS fallback.** If RCS cannot be delivered, send a normal SMS using an approved DLT template.
7. **Run a small pilot.** Send to a test group across Android and iPhone, and compare delivery and response with your SMS results.
8. **Measure and expand.** Check delivery, clicks and replies, and cost per outcome, before you scale.

## Good first uses for RCS

- Delivery and order tracking with a map or status button
- Appointment reminders with confirm and reschedule buttons
- Product carousels for a returning customer who has opted in
- Support conversations with quick-reply options
- Offers with a clear "buy" or "book" button

- ✅ An SMS fallback with an approved template is in place

## Common mistakes

- **Treating RCS as a replacement for SMS.** It is an addition, with SMS as a safety net.
- **Assuming all customers can receive it.** Phone, operator and settings all matter.
- **Planning around old prices.** Pricing changed in 2026 and may change again.
- **Sending promotional RCS without consent.**
- **Overdesigning.** Heavy carousels with long text perform worse than clear, short messages.
- **Skipping the pilot.**

## FAQ

**Is RCS available on iPhone in India?**
Business RCS has been available to iPhone users on Jio since September 2026, based on industry reports. Availability on other networks depends on the operator.

**Will RCS replace SMS?**
Not soon. SMS still reaches almost every phone, so most businesses use RCS alongside it.

**Is RCS cheaper than WhatsApp?**
It depends on the use case, operator and current price lists. Compare the real cost per outcome with your provider.

**Do I need DLT registration for RCS?**
SMS fallback needs DLT registration. RCS has its own verification and operator rules. Ask your provider what applies. Our [DLT registration guide]({{ '/2026/11/17/dlt-registration-sms-india-step-by-step/' | relative_url }}) covers the SMS side.

**How long does setup take?**
It depends on verification and operator approvals. Plan for some weeks, and ask your provider for current timelines.
