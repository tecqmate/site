## The Multi-Million Dollar Factory Gem-Set Grail

The **Patek Philippe Nautilus Reference 5723/112R-001** in 18k rose gold is one of the most exclusive factory gem-set sports timepieces in modern horology. Featuring a sunburst brown dial with horizontal embossed ridges, the watch is distinguished by its factory bezel channel-set with 32 flawless baguette-cut natural rubies (approx. 4 carats), complemented by baguette ruby hour markers at 6, 9, and 12 o'clock.


Reserved almost exclusively for Patek Philippe's most prominent global clients, secondary market acquisition is the only avenue for most collectors to secure this reference. However, trading at multi-million dollar valuations creates extreme incentive for illicit dealers to present aftermarket gem-set 5711 conversions as authentic factory 5723 pieces.

---

## Aftermarket Ruby Swaps and Misrepresented Base References
* **What is the problem?** Unscrupulous secondary dealers take standard rose gold 5711/1R models, cut the bezel, and install aftermarket rubies or synthetic stones to impersonate a factory 5723/112R-001.
* **Why does it happen?** The price delta between a standard rose gold Nautilus and a genuine factory gem-set Reference 5723 exceeds seven figures. Detecting custom gem settings requires forensic gemological auditing and certificate verification.

## A 4-Step Secondary Market Buying Protocol

If you are committing high-tier capital to acquire a Patek Philippe 5723/112R-001, follow this buyer-protection workflow:

* **Step 1: Require Guarantee Card Verification:** Ensure the original Patek Philippe Certificate of Origin explicitly lists the full reference code `5723/112R-001`. Never accept verbal claims of "factory settings" without matching paperwork.
* **Step 2: Mandate Independent Gemological & Horological Escrow:** Utilize specialized escrow vaults where an accredited gemologist verifies that all 32 bezel rubies and dial markers are natural, unheated gems set by Patek Philippe workshops.
* **Step 3: Audit Lug & Clasp Hallmarks Under Loupe:** Inspect the 18k 750 gold hallmarks, St. Bernard dog head, and Patek Philippe crown stamps on the caseback and fold-over clasp to verify deep, crisp laser outlines.
* **Step 4: Perform Serial & Archive Cross-Check:** Confirm that the movement and case serial numbers match official factory archives and boutique delivery records.
