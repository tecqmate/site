When Rolex introduced the **Day-Date 40 Reference 228235JG** crafted in proprietary Jubilee Gold paired with a natural **Light Green Aventurine Dial** and factory baguette-cut diamond hour markers, it immediately became one of the most coveted modern President references in high horology. Natural stone dials inherently possess unique micro-inclusions and shimmering mineral flakes, meaning no two aventurine dials are identical.

With boutique availability restricted strictly to top-tier VIP client allocations, secondary market acquisition is the primary path for collectors looking to secure this piece. ## 2. Aftermarket Dial Swaps and Synthetic Aventurine
* **What is the problem?** Unscrupulous secondary dealers often take standard President models, remove the factory dial, and fit third-party aftermarket aventurine dials or re-set non-factory diamonds onto basic indices to demand massive market premiums.
* **Why does it happen?** The price delta between a standard Day-Date 40 dial and a factory stone dial with baguette diamond markers can exceed $100,000. Detecting aftermarket stone dials requires forensic inspection under ultraviolet light and high magnification.

## 4. A 4-Step Secondary Market Buying Protocol

If you are acquiring a high-jewelry Rolex Day-Date 40 Reference 228235JG, enforce this strict buyer-protection workflow:

* **Step 1: Verify the Reference Suffix "JG" on Original Guarantee Card:** Confirm that the factory guarantee card explicitly lists the reference as `228235JG` (denoting factory stone dial and gem-setting) rather than a standard `228235`.
* **Step 2: Require Third-Party Gemological & Horological Escrow:** Never wire direct payment without independent inspection. Ship the timepiece to an accredited gemologist to confirm factory diamond settings and authentic natural aventurine mineral composition.
* **Step 3: Inspect Caseback & Lug Hallmarks Under Loupe:** Ensure the 18k gold hallmarks (St. Bernard dog head, 750 scales, and Rolex Crown) stamped behind the lugs feature deep, laser-crisp outlines rather than soft, buffed-out indentations.
* **Step 4: Demand Full Set Provenance & Box:** Ensure the presentation set includes the double-boxed Rolex green presentation case, serial-matched guarantee card, green seal, and all President bracelet links.
