## High-Tech Titanium Meets Vibrant Pop Aesthetics

The **Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Offshore Selfwinding Chronograph 42mm Reference 26238TI** represents a striking evolution of the classic 1993 Offshore silhouette. Crafted in lightweight grade 5 titanium with a sandblasted and satin-brushed case, this reference stands out with its bold **turquoise blue dial and yellow sub-dial accents**, vibrant rubber pushers, and matching integrated rubber strap.

Because boutique availability is strictly allocated to established AP clients, secondary market acquisition is the fastest route for collectors. However, with demand driving significant market premiums, buyers must navigate high-grade replica composite cases, repainted aftermarket dials, and cloned chronograph movements.

## Aftermarket Dial Repaints and Modified Base Movements
* **What is the problem?** Counterfeiting operations take standard grey or black dial Offshore references, repaint the *Méga Tapisserie* grid in turquoise with yellow accents, and present them as rare factory colorways.
* **Why does it happen?** The price delta between a standard secondary Offshore and a rare factory vibrant colorway can reach tens of thousands of dollars, incentivizing illicit dealers to perform third-party dial conversions.
