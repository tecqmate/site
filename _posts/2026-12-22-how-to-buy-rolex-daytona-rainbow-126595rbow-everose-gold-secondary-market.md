## The Holy Grail of Factory Gem-Set Chronographs

The **Rolex Oyster Perpetual Cosmograph Daytona Rainbow Reference 126595RBOW** in 18k Everose gold represents the pinnacle of factory high-jewelry watchmaking. Anchored by a fixed bezel set with 36 baguette-cut sapphires in a seamless rainbow gradient, this iconic reference is further embellished with 11 baguette sapphire hour markers matching the bezel's color spectrum and 56 brilliant-cut diamonds across the case lugs and crown guards.

Trading at multi-million dollar valuations on the global secondary market, the "RBOW" Daytona is one of the most counterfeited high-jewelry timepieces in history. The majority of listings encountered online are third-party custom jobs where aftermarket gem-setters modify standard Everose gold Daytonas.

## Aftermarket "Custom Rainbow" Conversions
* **What is the problem?** Dishonest dealers take standard Everose gold Daytona models (like Ref. 116505 or 126505), drill the lugs, swap the bezel, and install aftermarket sapphires and diamonds to impersonate a factory 126595RBOW.
* **Why does it happen?** A factory-original 126595RBOW commands upwards of $500,000 to $1,000,000+ depending on provenance, whereas a converted base model costs a fraction to build, creating immense profit motivation for fraud.

If you are committing seven-figure capital to acquire a factory Rolex Rainbow Daytona, enforce this strict buyer-protection workflow:

* **Step 1: Perform Guarantee Card Reference & Suffix Audit:** Ensure the official Rolex guarantee card explicitly displays the `RBOW` suffix. Never accept verbal claims that a watch is "factory set" if the papers cite a standard base model reference.
* **Step 2: Mandate Independent Gemological & Horological Escrow Inspection:** Ship the watch to an accredited vault where a master gemologist inspects the 36 bezel sapphires and 56 lug diamonds under high magnification to verify natural origin, flawless clarity, and factory channel-setting techniques.

* **Step 4: Audit Calibre 4131 Movement & Case Hallmarks:** Verify the openworked rotor (or Calibre 4130 for 116595) and inspect the deep St. Bernard dog head and 750 hallmark engravings under the lugs.
