## Low-Grade Figurines and Fake Flying Tourbillons
* **What is the problem?** Counterfeiting workshops produce replica Concept cases fitted with low-quality resin or stamped alloy Black Panther figures and non-functional tourbillon cages.
* **Why does it happen?** Crafting the authentic 3D figure requires 30 hours of laser carving, hand-engraving, and painting by a single artisan. Illicit producers attempt to bypass this labor cost to capture massive resale profit margins.
