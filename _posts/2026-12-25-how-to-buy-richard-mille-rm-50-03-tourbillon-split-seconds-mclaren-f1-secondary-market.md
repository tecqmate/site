Limited to just 75 pieces globally, the **Richard Mille RM 50-03 Tourbillon Split-Seconds Chronograph McLaren F1** is an absolute marvel of extreme material science and high horology. Weighing an astonishing **40 grams including the strap** (with the movement itself weighing a mere 7 grams), the RM 50-03 was developed in collaboration with the University of Manchester and McLaren F1.

Its revolutionary case is constructed from **Graph TPT**—a composite incorporating graphene, a nanomaterial six times lighter and 200 times stronger than steel. Trading at seven-figure valuations on the private secondary market, this ultra-rare reference requires exhaustive forensic auditing to avoid standard Carbon TPT counterfeits, non-functional split-seconds mechanisms, and altered movement components.

## Standard Carbon Swaps and Non-Functional Split-Seconds Mechanics
* **What is the problem?** Fraudulent secondary dealers take replica Carbon TPT cases, apply fake McLaren branding stickers, and install standard automatic chronograph movements lacking true split-seconds (rattrapante) functionality.
* **Why does it happen?** True Graph TPT composite production and ultra-lightweight grade 5 titanium/Carbon TPT movement skeletonization require millions in R&D. Counterfeit operations cut corners with heavy resin plastic or basic carbon fiber to pass off fake builds as 40-gram hyperwatch masterpieces.
