## Acrylic/Glass Shells and Dummy Cable Suspensions
* **What is the problem?** Counterfeiting operations manufacture clear plastic, PMMA acrylic, or low-grade mineral glass case bodies, pairing them with non-functional static wires and PVD-coated alloy movements to simulate the RM 56-02's complex cable architecture.
* **Why does it happen?** True factory sapphire crystal machining destroys dozens of diamond-tipped tools and carries an immense failure rate during production. Replica factories substitute soft acrylic or glass to mimic the visual transparency at a tiny fraction of the cost.
