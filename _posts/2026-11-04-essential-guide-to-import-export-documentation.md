<section class="mb-5">
  <h2>1. Introduction to Cross-Border Trade Workflows</h2>
  <p class="fs-6">International trade requires precise coordination between buyers, sellers, freight forwarders, and regulatory bodies. Ensuring smooth clearance relies on accurate paperwork, standardized commercial descriptions, and strict adherence to local import-export laws.</p>
</section>

<section class="mb-5">

        <tr>

        <tr>

<section class="mb-5">
  <h2>3. Key Compliance & Regulatory Steps</h2>
  <h3 class="h5">Harmonized System (HS) Classification</h3>
  <p>Assigning correct numerical HS codes to products is vital for determining applicable import duties, taxes, and regulatory restrictions before dispatching cargo.</p>
  
  <h3 class="h5">Customs Declarations</h3>
  <p>Exporters and importers must file electronic declarations with respective national customs portals, ensuring values match financial settlement records (such as letters of credit or wire remittances).</p>
</section>

<section class="mb-4">
  <h2>4. Conclusion</h2>
  <p class="fs-6">Maintaining organized records and verifying documentation guidelines prior to shipment minimizes customs delays, port storage penalties, and clearance friction.</p>
</section>
