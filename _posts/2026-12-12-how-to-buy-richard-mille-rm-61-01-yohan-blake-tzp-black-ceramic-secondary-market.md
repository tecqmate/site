## 1. The Asymmetric Aerodynamic Beast

Designed in collaboration with Olympic sprinter Yohan Blake, the **Richard Mille RM 61-01 Yohan Blake** is renowned for its distinctive **asymmetric case design**. Tapering in thickness and width between 9 o'clock and 3 o'clock, the case architecture is engineered to reduce aerodynamic drag and prevent the crown from rubbing against an athlete's wrist during high-velocity sprints.

Combining a ultra-tough **TZP (Tetragonal Zirconia Polycrystal) black ceramic** front and back bezel with a central case middle crafted from carbon, the RM 61-01 commands high six-figure valuations on the secondary market. However, because of its popularity among modern collectors, the market is rife with high-clone composite cases and modified standard movements.

---

## 2. Counterfeit Ceramic Finishing and Cloned Skeleton Movements
* **What is the problem?** Counterfeiting workshops utilize cheap black PVD-coated steel or low-grade resin composite to replicate the matte finish of TZP ceramic and the layered striations of carbon.
* **Why does it happen?** The price delta between a replica case assembly and an authentic RMUL2 skeletonized movement with Grade 5 titanium bridges generates massive financial incentive for fraudulent secondary dealers.

If you are committing six-figure capital to acquire an RM 61-01 Yohan Blake, enforce this strict buyer-protection protocol:

* **Step 1: Perform NFC Card & Digital Warranty Scan:** Validate the encrypted NFC warranty card directly with an official Richard Mille boutique or authorized service center database.
* **Step 2: Inspect Hand-Painted Green & Yellow Flanges:** Examine the inner hand-painted green and yellow upper bridges (nodding to the Jamaican flag) under 10x magnification to confirm clean, sharp hand-applied lines without paint bleed or color fading.
* **Step 3: Require Physical Inspection by a Master Watchmaker:** Never complete direct wire transfers. Require independent physical escrow where an accredited watchmaker disassembles the case to inspect the grade 5 titanium spline screws and dual-barrel movement torque.
* **Step 4: Audit Caseback Hallmarks & Provenance Set:** Verify that the caseback serial number matches the original guarantee folder, warranty card, and leather presentation box set.
