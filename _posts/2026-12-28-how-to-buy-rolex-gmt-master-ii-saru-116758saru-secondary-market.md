## The Original Gem-Set GMT Icon

Introduced as an off-catalog masterpiece to celebrate the 50th anniversary of the GMT-Master line, the **Rolex GMT-Master II SARU Reference 116758SARU** in 18k yellow gold is one of the most recognizable high-jewelry tool watches ever produced. Its name "SARU" derives from its bi-directional rotating bezel set with **18 baguette blue sapphires, 18 baguette rubies, 23 baguette diamonds, and 1 triangular diamond** at 12 o'clock—recreating the iconic "Pepsi" blue and red colorway using precious gemstones.

Because factory SARU models command massive six-figure premiums on the global secondary market, dishonest dealers frequently modify standard 18k yellow gold GMT-Master II models (such as Ref. 116710 or 116718LN) with third-party gem-set bezels and custom pavé lugs.

## Custom "SARU-Style" Bezel Conversions and Lug Modifications
* **What is the problem?** Unscrupulous grey-market brokers take standard yellow gold GMT-Master II references, install aftermarket sapphire/ruby/diamond bezels, and drill/set diamonds into the lugs and crown guards to imitate an authentic factory 116758SARU.
* **Why does it happen?** The value difference between a standard yellow gold GMT-Master II and a genuine factory 116758SARU can exceed $100,000 to $200,000, creating strong financial incentives for aftermarket alterations.
