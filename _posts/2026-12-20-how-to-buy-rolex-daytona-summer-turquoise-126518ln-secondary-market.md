The **Rolex Oyster Perpetual Cosmograph Daytona Summer Reference 126518LN** in 18k yellow gold is one of the most vibrant off-catalog executions in modern watchmaking. Combining an 18k yellow gold Oyster case with a black Cerachrom ceramic bezel, this reference stands out with its striking **vibrant turquoise blue dial** paired with contrasting black chronograph sub-dials and Oysterflex elastomer strap.

## Repainted Dials and Cloned Oysterflex Assemblies
* **What is the problem?** Fraudulent secondary dealers strip standard black or champagne dial 126518LN models, apply aftermarket turquoise lacquer printing, and market them as rare factory editions.
* **Why does it happen?** The price delta between a standard yellow gold Oysterflex Daytona and an authentic factory turquoise dial reference can exceed $30,000 to $50,000, tempting dishonest dealers into third-party dial conversions.

## A 4-Step Secondary Market Buying Protocol

If you are committing six-figure capital to acquire the Daytona 126518LN Turquoise, follow this strict buyer-protection workflow:

* **Step 1: Perform Rolex NFC Guarantee Card Audit:** Verify that the serial-matched digital warranty card explicitly carries the correct factory dial designation code (`126518LN-0014` or equivalent off-catalog serial matching).
* **Step 2: Mandate Physical Inspection via Independent Master Watchmaker:** Never accept photos as final proof. Utilize an accredited third-party watchmaker to inspect the caseback seals, movement serial numbers, and Calibre 4131 cut-out rotor.
* **Step 3: Audit Oysterflex Strap Internal Metal Blades:** Inspect the inner flexible titanium-nickel alloy blade structure of the Oysterflex strap. Authentic straps feature pristine flex resistance and clean size codes (e.g., D-E, E-F) stamped on the underside.
* **Step 4: Verify Full Set Presentation & Box:** Ensure the set includes the original Rolex green box, serial-matched warranty card, green chronometer tag, user manual, and matching Oysterflex clasp assembly.
