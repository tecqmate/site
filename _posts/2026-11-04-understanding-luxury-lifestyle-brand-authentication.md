<section class="mb-5">
  <h2>3. Modern Verification and Traceability Technologies</h2>
  <h3 class="h5">Digital Serialisation and NFC Chips</h3>
  <p>Many modern premium manufacturers embed Near Field Communication (NFC) tags, cryptographic serial numbers, or RFID threads directly into items, allowing buyers to verify production provenance against secure digital ledgers.</p>
  
  <h3 class="h5">Independent Authentication Standards</h3>
  <p>Professional authentication relies on microscopic inspection of material fibers, font kerning on date codes or heat stamps, and metallurgical analysis of hardware alloys rather than relying solely on paper certificates.</p>
</section>
