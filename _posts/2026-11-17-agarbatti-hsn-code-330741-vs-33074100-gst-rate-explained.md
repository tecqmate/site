Two codes show up for agarbatti: **330741** and **33074100**. They look different but describe the same product. Using the right one in the right place avoids invoice errors, customs queries, and wrong GST. This guide explains the difference and the current GST position.

*Rates were checked on November 17, 2026 against published summaries of the GST notifications. Rules change, so confirm with your CA or the latest notification before filing. This is general information, not tax advice.*

## 330741 vs 33074100: what is the difference?

| Code | Digits | Where it fits |
|---|---|---|
| **330741** | 6 | The international Harmonized System (HS) subheading. Used in global trade statistics and by many foreign buyers. |
| **33074100** | 8 | India's tariff item (ITC-HS). Used on shipping bills, bills of entry, and in customs. Also the code under which GST rates are listed as 3307 41 00. |

The first four digits (**3307**) are the heading for pre-shave, bath, deodorant, and other perfumery and room-freshening preparations. The 8-digit code narrows it to "agarbatti and other odoriferous preparations which operate by burning", which also covers things used during religious rites.

**Rule of thumb:** use the full 8-digit code **33074100** for Indian customs documents, and quote 330741 when a foreign buyer asks for the 6-digit HS code. How many digits you must show on a GST invoice depends on your turnover, so check the current rule for your business.

## What GST rate applies?

Published summaries of the GST schedules show two different rates under the same code 3307 41 00:

| Product | GST rate |
|---|---|
| Agarbatti, lobhan, dhoop batti, dhoop, and sambrani | **5%** (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST) |
| Other odoriferous preparations that operate by burning | **18%** |

The 5% rate for agarbatti has been in place since 2017 and was reported unchanged when GST rates were revised on September 22, 2025. The 18% rate for the "other" category applies to products outside the listed agarbatti and dhoop items. Packaging or branding alone does not generally change the rate for the listed products.

If your product is not clearly one of the named items (for example a new type of burning incense product), get the classification confirmed before you price or invoice it.

## Exports: use the 8-digit code and know the options

* Put **33074100** on the shipping bill and export invoice.
* Exports are generally treated as zero-rated supplies. You can supply under a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) without paying IGST, or pay IGST and claim a refund. Ask your CA which route suits your cash flow.
* Keep the HS description consistent across invoice, packing list, and shipping bill.

Trade data shows exports under 33074100 were around ₹1,285 crore in FY 2024-25, with steady growth over the last several years, so buyers and customs officers see this code often.

## Common mistakes

1. **Using only the 6-digit code on a shipping bill** when the 8-digit code is required.
2. **Charging 18% on listed agarbatti or dhoop**, or 5% on a product that actually falls under the "other" entry.
3. **Different descriptions on different documents.** Mismatches between invoice, packing list, and shipping bill cause queries.
4. **Copying an old code or rate** from an older invoice after a rate revision.
5. **Assuming a branded or packed product has a different code.** For the listed items, it generally does not.

## Quick checklist

* Identify your exact product (agarbatti, dhoop, sambrani, or something else).
* Use 33074100 for customs, and 330741 for foreign buyers who ask for HS.
* Apply the correct GST rate for that product, and keep a note of the notification you relied on.
* Keep descriptions identical across all export documents.
* Review rates whenever GST rates are revised.

## FAQ

**Is 3307 41 00 the same as 33074100?**
Yes. It is the same 8-digit code written with spaces.

**Does a different fragrance change the code?**
Generally no. The code depends on the type of product, not the scent.

**Do I need an IEC to export?**
Yes. Exporters need an Importer-Exporter Code from DGFT.
