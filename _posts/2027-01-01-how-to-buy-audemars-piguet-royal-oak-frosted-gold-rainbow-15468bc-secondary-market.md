## Aftermarket "Frosted" Conversions and Fake Gem Bezels
* **What is the problem?** Unscrupulous secondary dealers take standard white gold Openworked Royal Oaks (or base 15450 models), apply coarse hand-tooling to simulate Frosted Gold, and install third-party rainbow sapphire bezels.
* **Why does it happen?** Genuine factory 15468BC models carry a massive market premium over standard references. The cost difference to modify a base watch with aftermarket stones and custom diamond-tool micro-hammering is minimal compared to the multi-hundred-thousand-dollar market valuation.
