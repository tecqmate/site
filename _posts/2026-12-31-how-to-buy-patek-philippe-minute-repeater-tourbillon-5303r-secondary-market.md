## Open-Worked Acoustic Architecture Meets High Horology

First introduced at the Watch Art Grand Exhibition in Singapore and later entering regular production, the **Patek Philippe Minute Repeater Tourbillon Reference 5303R-001** in 18k rose gold is a historic departure for Geneva's most conservative manufacture. For the first time in Patek Philippe history, the minute repeater's striking hammers and gongs are exposed directly on the dial side, allowing the wearer to observe the mechanical choreography as it strikes the hours, quarters, and minutes.

Framed by a 42mm 18k rose gold case with white gold side inserts featuring intricate foliage marquetry, the 5303R is powered by the manual-wind Calibre R TO 27 PS. Because allocation requires personal approval from Thierry Stern, secondary-market pieces trade at multi-million dollar valuations. Acquiring one requires rigorous auditing to avoid modified cases, flat acoustic reverberations, and unverified archive provenance.
